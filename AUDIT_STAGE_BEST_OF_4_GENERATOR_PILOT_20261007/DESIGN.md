# DESIGN: Stage A Best-of-4 Generator Pilot (Zara Scene-Family Outer Split)

## 1. Scientific Context & Hypothesis
- **Context**: Prior audits confirmed:
  1. Oracle continuous parameters $z \in \mathbb{R}^8$ are identifiable and non-degenerate ($D_\tau = 0.0055\text{ m}$, Degenerate Rate = 3.79%);
  2. Past history exhibits strong conditional multimodality (77.25% of Change samples diverge into $\ge 3$ distinct maneuver classes given near-identical past);
  3. Single-point deterministic parameter regression and Gaussian MDN on single oracle labels $z^*$ failed on Change (-2.37 pp and -2.53 pp) due to conditional mean smoothing.
- **Control**: Static-K24 (Native 20 frozen + 4 static heuristic proposals: Decelerate, Stop, Accelerate, Reverse / Mild Turns).
- **Treatment**: Bo4-K24 (Native 20 frozen + 4 learned Motion Program proposals generated by a Best-of-4 generator trained directly on trajectory space $\min_{m} \mathcal{L}_{\text{traj}}(\tau(z_m), GT)$).
- **Candidate Budget**: Strictly $K=24$ (20 Native + 4 Bo4 slots).
- **Hypothesis**: Direct trajectory-space Best-of-4 training allows the past P2 representation to produce 4 distinct physical hypotheses without regression-to-the-mean collapse on a single pseudo-target, thereby increasing future coverage on Change samples ($> 41.71\%$) and generating net rescue.
- **Falsification Criteria**:
  1. Change Coverage fails to improve ($\le 41.71\%$);
  2. Change $\min\text{ADE}$ and $\min\text{FDE}$ both fail to improve;
  3. 4 slots exhibit severe collapse ($d_{\text{traj}} < 0.1\text{ m}$ or single slot dominance $> 75\%$);
  4. Improvements are confined to Stable while Change degrades.

## 2. Experimental Protocol
- **Dataset**: Zara scene-family protocol (Train $N=16,337$, Outer $N=1,561$, Change $N=211$).
- **Representation**: P2 (F16 Encoder output mean tokens, 96 dims).
- **Loss**: $\mathcal{L} = \min_{m \in \{1,2,3,4\}} (\text{ADE}(\tau(z_m), GT) + 0.5 \cdot \text{FDE}(\tau(z_m), GT)) + \lambda_{\text{div}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{div}}$.
- **Seed**: Preregistered Seed 42 (Stage A Pilot).
